04 Type --krk-font-sans · --krk-font-hand · the xs → 3xl ladder

05 The pen --krk-stroke-ui / -icon / -hero · .krk-boil · .krk-crayon

One pen for the whole system: stroke only, round caps and joins, cubic curves, no perfect circles and no ruler-straight lines. The tremble is baked into the path coordinates themselves — 2–4% of the canvas — and is never faked with a filter laid over exact geometry.

Boil and crayon

The boil displaces the line by about two pixels, so on a small motif there is simply nothing to see — draw it big, or don't promise it at all. The crayon grain lives only on the 200×200 hero canvas: at icon size it reads as noise rather than as texture.

09 Motifs doodles/ · viewBox 0 0 48 48 · stroke 2.8 · currentColor

The field

A field, not a table: the drawings are larger than their own cells and so cross one another, while all of them stand on one ground line. Where they overlap, the closed subpaths are filled with paper — a leaf, a cap, a berry stops being see-through because it lies on the paper, not because it was coloured in. A stem and a bristle never close, so they take no fill: that is read off the drawing itself, which is why a new motif arrives already correct.
